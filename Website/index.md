I study how microbes and the plants and animals that host them shape one another,
from fluorescent bacteria that influence aphid behaviour to the yeasts that travel
with a burying beetle. I combine fieldwork, microbiology, and bioinformatics
to ask evolutionary questions about everyday symbioses. Alongside that research I
teach undergraduate biology and microbiology, and supervise students through
research projects of their own.
