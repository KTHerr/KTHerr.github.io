{% comment %} Study-systems cladogram. Topology follows current consensus for the taxa actually sampled in this work: Bacteria (Alpha- + Gammaproteobacteria) vs Eukaryota; within eukaryotes, Fungi sister to Insecta; within Insecta, Coleoptera sister to Hemiptera. Tips are ordered with the insects at the top, reading down to the bacteria. The branch SVG is pure horizontal/vertical rules, so it is stretched with preserveAspectRatio="none" — a rectangular cladogram carries no branch-length information, and right angles stay right angles under that transform. Tip rows are HTML, so the labels stay real selectable text at true proportions. Geometry: viewBox is 560 x 500 for five tips, so each tip row is 100 units tall and tip centres fall at y = 50, 150, 250, 350, 450 — matching the five equal-height rows of .clado-tips. {% endcomment %}
